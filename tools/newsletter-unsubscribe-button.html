Unsubscribe button for the newsletter

Put this at the bottom of every newsletter you send. It takes readers to peevishpenman.com/unsubscribe, where they enter their email and are taken off the sign-up sheet straight away.

Unsubscribe

You’re getting this because you signed up at peevishpenman.com.

Copied — paste it into your email.

How to use it in Gmail

  1. Click Copy button above. (Or select everything inside the dashed box and copy it.)
  2. In your newsletter draft, click at the very bottom and paste.
  3. Put all subscribers in Bcc, never To or Cc, so readers can’t see each other’s addresses.

The sign-up sheet is partials/data/newsletter-subscribers.csv on the server. Download a fresh copy right before each send so anyone who unsubscribed since last time is already gone.
